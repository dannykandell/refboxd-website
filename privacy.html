Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

RefBox'd ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you use the RefBox'd mobile application and related services.

Information We Collect

When you use RefBox'd, we may collect information including:

  • Account information, such as your name, username, and email address.
  • Profile information and profile photos you choose to provide.
  • Officiating game logs, including sports, teams, schools, venues, dates, scores, levels, and positions.
  • Photos you choose to upload to your game logs.
  • Friend connections and other interactions within the app.
  • Device information necessary to provide features such as push notifications.

How We Use Information

We use information collected through RefBox'd to:

  • Provide and maintain the RefBox'd service.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Store and display your officiating history.
  • Generate statistics based on your game logs.
  • Provide social features such as friends and shared activity.
  • Send notifications related to activity within the app.
  • Improve the functionality, reliability, and user experience of RefBox'd.

Privacy and Sharing

RefBox'd is designed with privacy in mind. Your information is not publicly visible simply because you create an account.

Certain information and activity may be visible to users you have connected with as friends, depending on the features of the app. Information about officials you work with is kept private and is not publicly displayed as part of another user's profile.

Photos

RefBox'd allows users to upload photos associated with their game logs. Photos are stored so they can be displayed within the app and used with features such as sharing game memories.

Third-Party Services

RefBox'd may use third-party service providers to operate portions of the service, including database hosting, authentication, file storage, notifications, and application infrastructure.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services to RefBox'd.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide RefBox'd and operate its features. Users may delete game logs and other content through available features within the application.

Account Deletion

Users can delete their RefBox'd account directly within the application. Deleting an account will permanently remove the user's account and associated personal information, subject to any information we are required to retain for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

RefBox'd is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.
