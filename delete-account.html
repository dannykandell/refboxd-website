Delete Your RefBox'd Account

RefBox'd users can permanently delete their account directly from the RefBox'd mobile app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open RefBox'd and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Tap the Settings icon.
  4. Scroll to the Account section.
  5. Tap "Delete Account."
  6. Confirm by selecting "Delete Permanently."

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes your RefBox'd account and your personal officiating logs.

Games that you created may remain in RefBox'd so that game information can continue to be used by other officials who have logged the same game. These remaining game records are not your personal officiating log.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account or need assistance deleting it, contact RefBox'd Support at YOUR-EMAIL.
